# Business Viability Protocol

## Deliverables

Produce:

- primary and secondary personas grounded in evidence;
- the current workflow and measurable pain;
- 3-6 KPIs with baseline or `unknown`, numeric target, measurement method, and window;
- viability risks and evidence gaps;
- an assessment across Impact, Reach, Frequency, Differentiation, Defensibility, and Feasibility.

Use only `Must do`, `Strong`, `Maybe`, or `Pass` for scores. Every score needs a rationale tied to repository evidence, a cited source, or a labeled inference.

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate market size, adoption, conversion, cost, or user demand.
- Treat an unknown baseline as a discovery requirement, not zero.
- Separate business desirability from technical feasibility.
- Include privacy, accessibility, compliance, and operational cost when they affect viability.
- Prefer a measurable hypothesis over a confident but untestable claim.
