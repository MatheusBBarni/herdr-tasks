# Independent Critique Protocol

Use real host delegation when available. Select 3-5 perspectives that create genuine tension: pragmatic engineering, architecture, security/privacy, product, devil's advocate, and first-principles framing.

## Phase 1: Opening positions

Give every advisor the same evidence ledger, constraints, options, and other advisor roles. Require 2-3 concise paragraphs ending with `Key Point:`.

## Phase 2: Tensions and rebuttals

Identify 2-4 real disagreements with stakes. Re-dispatch opposing advisors and require each to:

1. steel-man the opposing position;
2. rebut it with evidence;
3. state `concede`, `partially concede`, or `hold firm`, with reason.

## Phase 3: Position evolution

Record initial position, final position, and what evidence caused any change. Do not erase dissent.

## Phase 4: Synthesis

Return:

- consensus;
- unresolved tensions and trade-offs;
- recommended V1;
- dissenting view;
- risks and mitigations;
- V1 exclusions;
- one V2+ stretch direction.

## Integrity rules

- Never present inline roleplay as independent advisor evidence.
- Do not give advisors different facts to manufacture disagreement.
- Re-run one out-of-character or unsupported response once; then disclose the failure.
- If delegation is unavailable, obtain user approval with lettered choices before substituting a labeled single-agent critique: `A. (Recommended) Proceed with labeled single-agent critique`, `B. Stop and wait for independent critique`, `C. Other`.
